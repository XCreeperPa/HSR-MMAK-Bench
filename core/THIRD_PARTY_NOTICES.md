# Third-party game material

Honkai: Star Rail / 崩坏：星穹铁道, its characters, game artwork, audio and video are associated with HoYoverse and their respective rights holders. This research archive reproduces 39 benchmark stimuli used in the experiment. Their rights remain with the respective rights holders; no ownership or official endorsement is claimed. This archive does not grant a CC BY or MIT license over underlying game media. Reusers must assess their own use under the applicable rights and permissions.

core/assets.json records available source descriptions, URLs, media IDs and SHA-256 values. Null/missing source URLs mean that no precise public URL was captured; these are not claims of permission. The first three images were provided by the author as official anniversary illustrations. Reference-answer citations document story sources. This release does not claim that a rights-holder redistribution agreement has been obtained.

Stimulus files are the compressed deployed versions actually used for testing, not reconstructed originals. Music uses the first ten seconds; video intervals and original source descriptions are retained where recorded. assets.json is authoritative for file identity; reference/source prose can contain historical naming mistakes and is preserved as recorded.
